# Team Workspace Launch Notes

We are launching **shared team workspaces** to pilot customers next month. This page captures the *decisions so far* and what is still open.

## Goals

- Let a team lead invite colleagues in **under one minute**
- Make it obvious which documents are *owned* and which are *shared*
- Keep permissions simple: **viewer** and **editor** only

## Rollout plan

1. Internal dogfood with the product team
2. Pilot with three customer teams
3. General availability after a two-week feedback window

### Risks

- Two people editing the same document at once (*last write wins* for now)
- Imported Word files with complex layouts may lose some styling
